# Frame Load Analyzer — agreed scope

Standalone Python/Streamlit app, importable solver and render_app(), sf_ UI keys, inch/lbf/psi. Existing tube stress/CG apps are unchanged. Deliver source ZIP, installation instructions, reusable examples and verification results.

Design sidebar starts with two layers and an Add beam layer button, with no fixed upper limit or count input. Each expandable layer has a Remove layer button; at least one layer remains. Removal deletes its rods, lifting points, blocks and additional loads and resets its coil bearing selections to Auto. Welded built-up members count as one layer; their components may have different longitudinal lengths, starts and section offsets. Presets: angle, HSS, C-channel, angle+angle, angle+channel, angle+HSS, any one preset+flat bar above/below, custom drawn or supplied properties. Geometry in section plane z/y, front frame x/y. Main members are automatically cut by all intersecting coil cylinders defined by coil centre x/y and outside radius. No duplicate cutout inputs. Remaining A, centroid, I, elastic moduli, estimated shear area and density-based weight vary along x. Rectangular blocks are welded, uncut rectangular contact pads, assigned to a parent beam, with x/y position, longitudinal length, transverse depth and vertical height. They add local section material, deadweight and contact surfaces. Invalid block/tube interference is reported rather than silently machining the blocks.

Rods: centre x, diameter/thread/TPI, welded beam, nut-on-top beam, effective free length and optional stiffness override. Hand-tight, zero preload, tension-only EA_t/L. Visible weld triangles and nut/washer at attachment. Intermediate pass-through is not a tie. Lifting points are assigned to selected beam with position/optional display hole diameter; no separate plates. Vertical force only, small-deflection planar bending, no chain tilt solution.

Loading: package coil weight, number of longitudinal frames, equal/manual frame share, equal/relative individual coil weights, load multiplier, automatic beam/block/rod deadweight. Optional additional point/distributed beam loads for general cases. Equal lifting-hole reactions checkbox: applies per frame, validates force/moment compatibility. Normal mode uses level vertical supports; rotation is free.

Material: CSA G40.21 44W frame preset, ASTM A193 B7 rod preset, density/E/nu/Fy/Fu editable. Actual material/weld connection acceptance not inferred from low nominal stress. B7 supplied material properties do not verify welded regions.

Main live frame schematic, section inspection at chosen x, CAD editor with line/polyline, rectangle, circle, arcs, fillets, numeric dimensions, move/copy/rotate/mirror/delete, add/subtract, snap/grid, zoom/pan, undo/redo. Closed profiles/voids, valid material preview; circles/arcs polygonized at controlled resolution. Save/reload model JSON and custom geometry.

Results tabs: Bending moment, Shear force, Deflection, Stress, Forces, Calculations. Critical labels only; beam selection/all beams. Calculations use equations -> substitutions -> boxed units, overall/member/coil/rod FBDs above related equations. Raw matrices/numerical details optional. Show variable section and local nominal stress, force/moment equilibrium, penetration and convergence. Local notch, lug, weld, block, tube wall and 3D strength outside beam model; no automatic safe-to-lift rating.

Previous numerical reports ignored coil-seat material removal. Their values and welded/unwelded comparison are not validation targets for cut frames.
